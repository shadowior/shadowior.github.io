Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at ShanghaiTech University, advised by Prof. Hao Geng, with expected graduation in 2027. I research AI for manufacturing EDA and computational lithography, spanning layout understanding, mask optimization and GPU-accelerated simulation. My experience includes algorithm development and validation projects in both semiconductor manufacturing and manufacturing EDA.

My long-term goal is to connect these stages through shared data, physics-aware models and full-flow validation, improving manufacturability and computational efficiency. I am particularly interested in how errors and constraints propagate across a process chain; agentic AI workflows are a future research direction toward cross-stage optimization.

Selected Projects

GPU-native 3D lithography simulation

N3Litho · DAC 2026 · First author, equal contribution · Paper

Co-developed a numerical-neural framework combining a GPU vectorial Abbe solver with neural 3D field reconstruction, supporting resist modeling and downstream OPC/ILT evaluation.

Validation: In the paper’s 22 nm setup, throughput reached 29.91 μm²/s versus 0.0186 μm²/s for the commercial reference, with 3D aerial-image MSE of 6.6 × 10−4.

Scaling vision models to full-chip mask optimization

LVM-MO · DAC 2025 · Co-first author · Paper

Co-developed a mask optimization model using geometry pretraining, differentiable lithography feedback and cross-scale training on approximately 5 million layouts, addressing long-range interactions and stitching errors.

Validation: Optimized 60 × 60 and 150 × 150 μm² layouts in 4 s and 28 s on one A100 GPU in the reported benchmark. Best Paper Award Nomination, DAC 2025.

Manufacturability-aware Manhattan mask generation

TokMan · NeurIPS 2025 · Co-first author · Paper

Co-developed a Manhattan mask optimization method based on geometric sequence generation, combining a Diffusion Transformer, differentiable lithography feedback and lightweight ILT refinement.

Validation: On ICCAD13-S, reduced EPE from 2.70 to 2.16 and average mask shot count from 4948.4 to 287.6 versus IL-ILT, evaluating print fidelity, mask-writing complexity and runtime together.

Structured layout understanding and reliable hotspot detection

LLM-HD · DAC 2024 · First author, equal contribution; HyDAS · IEEE TCAD · First author · LLM-HD / HyDAS

Developed hotspot-detection methods: LLM-HD preserves geometry through GDS semantic encoding; HyDAS combines spatial/spectral features, deformed attention and selective prediction to support engineer review of high-risk patterns.

Validation: LLM-HD raised mean hotspot recall from 91.99% to 93.99% versus DATE’22 on the reported benchmarks and received a DAC 2024 Best Paper Award Nomination. HyDAS includes the public ICCAD2020 dataset.

Multimodal pretraining for label-efficient hotspot detection

IEEE TCAD 2026 · Second author · Paper & dataset

Contributed to multimodal pretraining research aligning layout images and GDSII text through contrastive learning and polygon-coordinate generation. The project uses 500K paired samples and tests low-resource adaptation and cross-design generalization to address labeling cost and model reuse.

Evaluating AI across the computational lithography flow

Ariadne’s Thread · ICCAD 2026, forthcoming · Co-author · Paper & dataset

Contributed to full-flow benchmark research connecting lithography modeling, OPC, EBL modeling and MPC, with aligned intermediate data and commercial-tool re-validation of composed pipelines.

Results: Over 104K aligned instances across 28/32/45 nm nodes and 18 method combinations. In 4 of 5 tested conditions, the best full-flow combination differed from the combination of stage-wise winners, motivating cross-stage optimization.
